1.FABRIC SELECTION
Fabrics provided by us are standardised ensuring same quality each time.
However, there can be slight differences in different rolls of the same fabric
in terms of feel as they are woven at different times.
The fabrics selected for printing, is the sole responsibility of the client. The
team can just suggest based on client inputs. To be sure of the fabric quality
, the client can order white samples or print 2 meters (MOQ) of the fabric,
before the main order to ensure the fabric quality .
2. TERMS OF DESIGN MAKING
The charges of the design making solely depends on the design and will be
given at the time of placing the order.
All the details required for making to be given at once, to avoid delays in
changes and confusion.
There are only 2 changes allowed after the design is made, in terms of sizes
or colours. The changes after which will be chargeable depending on the
change required
The print files will be shared by us through links that are downloadable. The
format of print file needed can be mentioned by the client at the time of
delivery.The client is supposed to download the file within 3 days, after
which we are not obliged to share again.
If the files are shared by client for printing , they should meet the
requirements of printing.
There is a strict rule we follow of non sharing of any of the clients designs,
and keeping them confidential.
3. SAMPLING OBLIGATIONS
Its always suggested to sample new designs before main print to ensure the
shades of print and the fabric quality. MOQ is 2 meters for any fabric
quality, and many designs can be tested within that.

4. PRINTING OBLIGATIONS
During printing process, fabrics are prone to shrinkage in length (varies
between 5-12%) as these are natural fabrics.
If the customer wants to add the shrinkage percentage in the order placed ,
we add 5% extra fabric in the invoice.This can be done when certain fixed
number of pieces are to be printed.
For printing of running fabrics the client may or may not opt for fabric
addition accounting shrinkage. Not taking into account shrinkage can lead to
less number of meters in the final outcome than actually ordered.

The order will be placed through an order form. After submission of which ,a

On confirming the order pdf, invoice will be shared.
50% of the order payment , is a confirmation to start the processing the

The design confirmation, in form of video will be taken before print after

Custom orders require patience and compassion from our clients, as we pour

So, keep a fair amount of buffer time for your deliveries ,and stock up your

7. COURIER SERVICES
We use reliable courier services( trackon and PNS) for shipping all over
India.
Courier charges(air shipping)-with 2-3 days delivery time are 80 INR for
first 500g after which every kg is charged 100 rupees.The client can ask for
one day shippings for which the rates differ substantially.
We do not provide free shipping , as our costings are low enough for any
small business that wants custom printing.
The courier charges are shared after the parcel moves from factory to

Failure of payment of courier charges will cancel the shipment and withhold

We don't entertain client courier pickups from factory as it disturbs our
pickup records and cause misplacement issues.
8.WASH INSTRUCTIONS
Normally digitally printed fabrics can be machine washed.
Incase of expensive fabrics that are hand washed, care should be taken to
rinse and spin dry the cloth before putting on the drying stand ,ensuring
there is no dripping water or soap left.
We are not responsible for any harsh detergents / bleaching agents/ harsh
perfumes /corrosive seawates.
Soaking is strictly prohibited.
Certain fabrics can have an inherent property of bleeding for a few washes,
but the colour will not stick to the cloth if dried properly.
The stitching of these fabrics should follow a general norm of stitching
garments keeping in mind when they wash , they can shrink a bit as garment,
especially when its cotton, linens ,or modal, to avoid awkward garment
lengths.
6. DAMAGE OR DEFECT TERMS
Weaving defects are common in natural fabrics like blob of fabric pile out of
the weave or a pull in the thread of the weave , these are common and
universally acceptable.
We try to control any damage during print, but there can be human errors, for
which 5% of damage in total order, for any order above 10 meters of the same
fabric is acceptable in textile industry, beyond that its subjected to scrutiny
and applicable for reprint/refund only for that meters.(Note : Damages have
noting to do with design or shades.)
Damage issue will be considered valid only when its reported within 24 hours
of delivery, proper videos of proof attached. We can ask for a video call during
validation as well.
7. GENERAL TERMS
We have no factory visits, as we have ID cards issued for people who can only
enter the factory for security reasons.
People who want to send their fabrics to factory for print, need to label the
fabrics correctly with name, amount of meters and the composition , to avoid
confusion and misplacement ,also share the tracking to help us receive them
correctly.
Remember even the customer fabrics are prone to shrinkage, so send fabrics
accordingly.We will share an account of left over fabric after print to help you
track your fabric.
